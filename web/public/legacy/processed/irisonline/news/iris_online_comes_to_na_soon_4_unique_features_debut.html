Iris Online Comes to NA Soon, 4 Unique Features Debut

The leading free-to-play online game publisher Gala-Net, Inc. has announced that they partnered with EYA SOFT Co. Ltd. to publish Iris Online in the North American market. Iris Online is a 3D MMORPG set in a fantasy world where three races, human, spirit, and half-beast co-exist. A highlight of Iris's features includes cute graphics, mountable monsters, and PvP combat. Before Iris Online landing in North America, let's take a sneak peek at its key features.
