Description of lunia

Lunia combines arcade and RPG.

It tries to find harmony between essential RPG elements such as character growth,community system and arcade game action.

The movement and fighting is all done with your keyboard.

Lunia has a stage System – Which lets you take on story quests one by one. Various modes such as the Main Stage, Side Dungeon Stage, and Raid Dungeon Stage with Raid Monsters.

The game engine combines 2-D background with 3-D characters.

Featuring the "Animatic Cinema System" with animation visual effects and lines that make up an important part of the stories.

Sound System – Over 10,000 Lunia Gold of real-life impact sounds for each object and material.Press the shortcut key for the ‘Voice Chat System’ to chat with words and the voices of actors for a life-like play.

And of course a PvP System.
