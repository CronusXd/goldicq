Atlus Online announces Pandora Saga

Atlus Online, publishers of NeoSteam: The Shattered Continent and Soul of the Ultimate Nation, have announced that Pandora Saga will be added to their line-up early next year.

Pandora Saga, currently published with a global version in Malaysia, is a large scale free-to-play MMORPG with a medieval theme. The game offers six races (Human, Elf, Dwarf, Myrine, Enkidu, and Lapin) and four basic classes (Warrior, Scout, Acolyte, and Mage) that split into a total of 28 classes. Features for the game include three-nation warfare, (Pandora Saga Gold)real-time action combat that rewards player tactics such as positioning, and PvP that supports up to 600 players on the battlefield at once.

To learn more, and to sign up for closed beta, visit the official Pandora Saga website.
