Aika Online to Enter Closed Beta Testing

Aika Online is an epic fantasy MMORPG developed by JoyImpact, creators of Neo Steam: The Shattered Continent. Five nations vie against one another for control of Arcan, waging war on a scale never before seen in an MMORPG. Nations hurl themselves against one another in massive castle sieges, relic thefts, and customized battlegrounds. Each nation is ruled by a player, the Lord Marshal, whose supreme power is supported by a regime of guilds.

Preeminence is an uncertain throne, however, as nations may ally together to usurp the ruling kingdom and guilds may conspire to overthrow the ruling council. Players take a pivotal role in this drama as they choose one of six classes, each of which undergoes multiple evolutions, and are assisted by their Pran—an intelligent, sassy fairy familiar which supports them in all their endeavors. Aika’s gameplay innovations have not gone unnoticed. The Korea Game Industry Agency (KoGIA) awarded Aika the prestigious Best of Show: Prime Minister’s Award, Player’s Choice: Best Online Game, and Best Game Design & Scenario.

Aika Online represents many tremendous advancements for PvP-centric MMORPGs. The game engine has been custom designed to support 2,000 aika gold players in a single, real-time battlefield—a remarkable technical achievement. Players choose to become a citizen of one of five nations, where they can siege each others’ capitol fortresses, raid rival nations’ enchanted relics, and compete in a variety of warfare scenarios. Not only must players manage these tactical objectives, but they must also remain vigilant of greater strategic goals. Players determine multinational alliances, the workings of their nations’ economies, and the governance of their citizens. Beneath these innovative elements, Aika contains the core features of a premier MMORPG: dungeon instances with multiple difficulty settings, raids, a comprehensive crafting system, and an immersive storyline with over 1,000 quests. We look forward to debuting Aika’s deep and unique world to Western players!” – Patrick Paik, Marketing Manager, Gala-Net, Inc.
