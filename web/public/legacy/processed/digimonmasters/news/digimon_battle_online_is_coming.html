Digimon Battle Online is Coming

Kotaku reported that WeMade Entertainment is going to bring Digimon Masters Online, an online virtual world where players battle their monsters. Judging by the date on the trailer below, it may likely be coming sometime next month.

Digimon Battle was used the name to license "Digimon RPG" for distribution in North America and China. Under the development of CDC Games, Digimon RPG was expected to be launched first in the Korean market last year. It is a game based on a series of very popular Japanese animated television shows called, "Digimon Adventure," "Digimon Adventure 02," "Digimon Tamers," and "Digimon Frontier." Digimon RPG2 (currently the tentative name with an official name to be decided prior to launch date) is a 3D MMORPG version of Digimon based on the series of TV shows following those listed above for Digimon RPG, (Digimon Masters Online Gold)as well as a popular series of shows called "Digimon Savers."

WeMade Entertainment Co., Ltd is an online games developer and service provider, based in Seoul, South Korea. They are the creators of the Legend of Mir series of MMORPGs, the two most successful being the The Legend of Mir 2, and its sequel The Legend of Mir 3. Legend of Mir has attracted over 120 million users in Asia, and generated over $65 million a month in revenue during its height. Even today, in its ninth year of operation, Legend of Mir 2 still generates well over $20 million a month in China alone.(From Wikipedia)
