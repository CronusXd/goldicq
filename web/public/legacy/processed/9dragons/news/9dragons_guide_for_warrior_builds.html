9Dragons: Guide for Warrior builds

Hello,guys! I have noticed that many unexperienced and experienced players in 9Dragons have questions about WARRIOR builds, so i I search a warriors build guide for all players. Hope it is usefull for you! Enjoy!

Str/Dex build:

In this build the player has to focus on max str ASAP to provide high def. also the player has to achieve the highest dmg as possible and to increase his dmgs in crits. ,dex affects att. rating and dodge att. rating basically controls how many hits u can land on the target and dodge controls how much ur opponent is gonna miss. , ess must be 14 which is the requirement for EC DEX relics and wis must be 8. Now how much cons and how much dex?if u are a high FC lvl u will be looking to have 170 to190 dex points and the rest to constitution.Mid cs are looking to have 190 to even maxed dex.

What can i do with this build?

Well this build is good for pvp ,since the player will be able to hit and crit a lot ,even though this build works only WITH WARRIOR VS WARRIOR, since low cons is the handicap, the user will have issues dueling hybrids and nukers ,also a proven fact is that str/cons build prevails most of the times in duels but thats another thing, its not really a good option for the player that wants to live in BP.This build is basically awesome to GRIND (PVE)its a pretty common build in high FC and CS players, i know some people that have max dex and compensate the lack of cons with gear .This build is also good to get epis at low lvl and out dmg ppl when killing bosses, etc..

Enhance this build

Make sure u have a 200 def trinket or 60 def relics, get a critical weapon, slotted, but preferably a 15 or 30 % refined one, ( if u can get an ec wd refined that will do if u got the guts lol) 4 pkts is a must ,def clothing such as slotted shoes or WB help a lot( use the talisman of Dr. Steelpipe), as well as dex relics(ec dex is a must), + 5 all seal, Shen mo is the right epi to use but KOG and punisher will rock since u will be criting a lot as well as raising ur dmg.att rating hat is a valuable asset as well, dodge hat will work nicely too. If ur rich and u can afford dmg plates(7% one) as well as att rating ones buyem .Premium pack will help a lot.DMG triagrams are good too, If u ever get TGW epi a blood bead and 300 dmgs u will be blessed too =P. Dun forget hp hat and conqueror trinket when grinding.
