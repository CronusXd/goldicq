Balanced Build:

This build requires the user to max str, have 14 in ess and 8 in wis, constitution must be over 100, and dex can be 140 to 150.(change acordingly to ur lvl)

What can i do with this build?

Well u can be a decent grinder and pvper, since u have the best of both worlds.There are no mayor weaknesses in this build, a well structured balanced build can do lots of things.Its pretty versatile since u can play a lot with stats, u can either turn it to a cons, or a dex build depending on the relics u use.If u turn it to cons, u can hit over 10 k hps or so.
