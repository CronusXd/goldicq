ARGO Gold features

Today Korea based Mgame launched official site for its developing title ARGO and prepared for the closed beta test that will start in 2011. They also released the world view, artworks and game interface for this game. In ARGO, two races survived from the battle made war to scramble for the Earthdium. Players could update their abilities and battle status, according to different resource distribution. Factions on battlefield, composing are random so that you can learn new stratagem in every time you joined the battle.

In ARGO Gold, gamers will play as heroes in ancient myths and take on adventures in various vehicles. This game integrates different times, styles and materials to present the audience a unique gaming environment.

ARGO, where past and future coexist, details the war between the two races - Noblian (human's last descendant which hides underground and survives) and Floresslah (mutant human) which evolves from human into a new race on the ground and survives.

ARGO Gold features tons of unique vehicles such as armored vehicles and enormous battleships, which allow players to quickly travel on the vast land and in the wide sky. Some of the vehicles have a capacity of 10+ people, while some can only accommodate 1 person. The one-man vehicle can be customized according to ARGO Online Gold players' preferences and become players' collections.
