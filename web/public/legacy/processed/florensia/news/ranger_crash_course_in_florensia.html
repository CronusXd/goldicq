Ranger Crash Course in Florensia

The Florensia Ranger Guide will tell you everything about the creation of your ranger and your ranger's skills. Furthermore you become familiar with soloing and grouping tactics for every level. The guide provides information on how to raid, which equipment fits best, which craft to choose and how to make the most money:

Highlights of the Florensia Gold Ranger Guide:

How to get from level 1 to 60 faster than any other class

Advanced grouping strategies for all levels

Ranger skill advice and hints

Starter friendly newbie guide including quest walk-throughs

An in-depth guide to heroic opportunities

Quest walk-throughs from tier 1 to 6

All you need to know about crafting for rangers

Raids: Strategies, scout tactics and equipment hints

How to make 75 gold per hour as a Ranger

You and Your Scout: This part is designed to get you well along the path to become a ranger. The Florensia Gold Ranger Guide includes tips on completing the milestones in your early levels quick and easy and a walk-through of the betrayal process in case you decide to play an evil-race ranger.

Florensia Gold Ranger Skills: When it comes to combat abilities your skills are most important. The ranger guide includes detailed lists about the skills your ranger gains while leveling up. It also shows how the skill lines change and increase when upgrading your skills.

Soloing and grouping: The Florensia Gold Ranger Guide provides level-by-level soloing and grouping tactics for your ranger from level 1 to 60. The fantastic soloing ability of the ranger class is one of Florensia Gold best-kept secrets. It takes quite an amount of effort to do it well, but when done right rangers really are one of the top soloing classes in the game.

Raiding: From an extensive list of raid encounters, their weapon immunities and where to find them to encounter specific walk-throughs, the Florensia Gold Ranger Guide features an in-depth raiding chapter. Find out which mobs are involved in the prismatic quests.

Heroic Opportunities: Scouts play an important role when it comes to HOs - therefore it is an absolute must to know the different types of HOs, which ones are best and which combinations of classes are required to perform a special HO. The Florensia Gold Ranger Guide presents all this in detailed tables and while furnishing you with tactical advice.
