More Surprises Besides Season2 Update

The announcement of Florensia Season 2 with exciting update was just the beginning. And more than you might have expected will follow bit by bit in the course of season 2! Today MMOsite took a interview with Simone from Florensia team to pry into the new contents of Florensia with you.

MMOsite: Hi, so glad to have an interview with you here, I have plenty of questions about your upcoming Florensia Season 2. Before this topic, can you give us a brief introduction about yourself?

Simone: My name is Simone and I am the Marketing Coordinator for Florensia Gold and other games at burda:ic. My field of work mostly relates to visual and written communication, as well as creative efforts centered on Florensia and the community. I have been part of the Florensia team at burda:ic from the very beginning, and I'm really happy about Season 2 and the overall development of our game.

MMOsite: How is this game going in 1 year's running? Can you tell us which part of this game attracts players the most?

Simone: Simone: It's been a great year. Florensia had a strong launch, and we've been constantly working to ensure our players are having fun. Between the elemental damage system, new islands, PvP War Zones, specialized classes, and everything else that's been added, Florensia has never been more exciting. We have also hosted numerous events and creative contests to provide our community with activities beyond the normal gameplay.

Many players are interested in fighting the strongest boss monsters and collecting the most powerful equipment in the game. Some love to write guides and play all the possible classes in order to share their experiences with the community. Others love to explore the Ocean and enjoy the trips through the beautiful countryside or chatting in the towns. It's hard to say what our players love the most because we have so many different types of players with different interests, but I'd say it's the general way of life in Florensia Money they all find appealing.

MMOsite: And the weakest part of this game? Do you have any plan to improve it?

Simone: Simone: The most common feedback we heard from players was that they wanted more PvP options, and we definitely think the War Zones and the upcoming Harbor Occupation Wars address this request. If they still want more after Season 2, we'll be happy to oblige! We're also constantly working to provide endgame and high-level content since the players never get tired of new challenges. More quests are currently being translated and will be added soon. Feedback from the community has always been and will always be very important to us as we evaluate what should be done next.
