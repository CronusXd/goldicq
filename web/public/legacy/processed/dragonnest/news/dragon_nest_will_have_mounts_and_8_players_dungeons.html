Dragon Nest will have Mounts and 8 Players Dungeons

Is Dragon Nest just an action game with endless dungeon combats? Do you think it will last long with the current system? In fact many players and media have asked the similar questions to the developer and got some exciting answers regarding to the future update of Dragon Nest, including mount and pet system, new advanced classes, new city, and 8 players dungeons!

New level, new city, 8 players Nests

Now the Korean servers have raised the level cap to 40. The next step is to introduce level 50 and then 60, where a brand new city Lotus Marsh (로터스마쉬) will be opened. New level cap means new dungeons. At present (at level 40), orange items is the best, but after the release of new update, purple items will come. 8 players Nests will be introduced after level 40 Apocalypse Nest.

 

Players can achieve the first advanced class at level 15. According to the developer, players will see the second advanced class after level 40, they are now making the physical form of these advanced classes and thinking the skills. It's told that these classes won't be revealed within this year. on the other hand, he confirms there won't be new basic class in the future.Dragon Nest Gold is useful in the game.

Mount & Pet

Mount will be an exciting thing, what's more, it's not in the item shop, as the lead designer says, but mount can be only used in the city for entertainment. This is the current plan. Compared to mount, pet will be more practical and this system is under development now. Pet can give its master buff.

On answering the PvP question from This is game.com, the director of Dragon Nest tells that they are planning to introduce guild vs. guild into arena. He says they will consider making exclusive maps for guild battles.

That's the information we collected from the interviews to the developer of Dragon Nest, and we'll bring you more details as the developer release more info.
