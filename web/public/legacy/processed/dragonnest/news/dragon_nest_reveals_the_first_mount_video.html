Dragon Nest Reveals the First Mount Video

In our last report about Dragon Nest we've mentioned some exciting things that will be introduced into the game, including the mount system. Now, we got a Dragon Nest mount demonstration video here and you can take a glance at the new feature. 

The mount riding video itself isn't shining compared to those combat videos but you can dig out something here. According to the information from Korean website, mounts can only be used in the city but on the screen there are battle points that only appear in the dungeons. Does it mean mounts could be available in the dungeon? Besides, you can jump over the obstacles when riding, something like Assassin's Creed...


 

Another new feature introduced into the game recently was the apprentice system. As more and more high level dungeons are brought in, casual and new players would feel hard to catch up with the others. Now they may be benefit from this system. Player under level 30 with Dragon Nest Gold can be apprentice and those who are above 30 can be masters, therefore, low level players can go into the high level dungeons with the masters and experience the exciting contents.
