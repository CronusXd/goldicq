Dragon Nest News & Events & Guides

Dragon Nest works like a lot of free-to-play

It lets you create a character from a specific class (such as warrior, wizard, archer, or priest) and then sally forth into instanced combat areas to beat the living heck out of various cutesy monsters by repeatedly clicking your mouse button to swing your weapon or occasionally using various hotkeyed abilities.

Instanced areas are divided into short, combat-heavy segments bounded by doors that unlock only (which lets you spend real money in microtransactions in exchange for in-game items) when Dragon Nest all monsters are destroyed, but these shorter segments make it easier for impromptu parties to stick together until they can take on the boss and eventually complete the instance and either move on to the next instance or return to a shared-space town to socialize with other players or purchase items from the in-Dragon Nest game cash-op.

Players can achieve the first advanced class at level 15. According to the developer, players will see the second advanced class after level 40, they are now making the physical form of these advanced classes and thinking the Dragon Nest Gold skills.

It’s told that these classes won’t be revealed within this year. on the other hand, he confirms there won’t be new basic Dragonnest Gold class in the future.

Mount will be an exciting thing, what’s more, it’s not in the item shop, Dragon Nest Gold as the lead designer says, but mount can be only used in the city for entertainment. This is the current plan. Compared to mount, pet will be more practical and this system is under development now. Pet can give its master buff.
On answering the Dragonnest Gold PvP question from This is game.com, the director of Dragon Nest tells that they are planning to introduce guild vs. guild into arena. He says they will consider making exclusive maps for guild battles.

That’s the information we collected from the interviews to the developer of Dragon Nest, and we’ll bring you more details as the developer release more Dragon Nest info.
