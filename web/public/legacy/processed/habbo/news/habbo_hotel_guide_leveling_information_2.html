Habbo Hotel Guide: Leveling Information

If you pick up Habbo Hotel Making at an early level with habbo credits, and don’t yet have the kind of capital, follow the advice of other posters and just let the profession level naturally with your current level. Renown gear can be used to supplement any skill breaks you experience.

Maybe this is the fastest way to get through the low level with habbo credits, unprofitable Habbo Hotels and start to craft the rare and epic quality Habbo Hotels that people will soon begin to desire greatly. It is not the most cost-effective, and will leave a dent in your wallet.

While to do this first you need to make sure you have at least 20g you’re willing to spend and find a ’Hedge Wizard’. They are the ones who will train you in the Habbo Hotel making habbohotel coins, unlike every other profession, which has an obvious name.

Hedge Wizards can be found in the first quest hub after your initial quest hub. For Chaos, it is the quest hub just north of the level 4 Public Quest. So, you get your first few rounds of quests in one camp, move down the road, and the second camp has the trainers. While you’re there train Magical Salvaging, or you’ll regret it later.
