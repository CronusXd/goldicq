AoE Information in Fiesta

Today we will introduce some AoE information in Fiesta to you , Maybe you have found this article is from the forum. we collect this guide there. Credits to the original author. Hope you like it ,If you have free time , just have a look at this article :

AoE- Area of Effect , It is a complicated system . With a good tank and cleric AoE is quite simple . As an archer you play a major role in the AoE party . Multi-shot is your first AoE skill . It is a very useful AoE skill though it may be a bit weak in some peoples'eyes . Also with the use of Nature's protection you are at the top of many AoE parties . You as an archer, have more defense then mages allowing you to be able to gather larger mobs .

You will use nature's protection right if you near the tank , When you gather the mobs . Have him and use devastate since you mostly AoE the higher the level you are . You may get hit a few times so a speed scroll is recommended to give a slight speed boost so you get hit less often . Then the best thing is to put a DoT on each monster you've pulled . Once that is done target the one the tank is killing and prepare to use multi-shot .

Multi-shot has been given a major makeover and is now a lot faster . This pay for the weak damage it gives . Every 7 seconds you can use this AoE . Though it may take little damage off over time the many times you use it , the damage builds up . It isn't better then what mages may have, but it is something quite useful. On top of the DoTs and other skills , the multi-shot is an ample AoE skill. Later when you hit level 51 you get an AoE poison skill . This allows you to stick a DoT on more then one mob. This also allows you to skip having to pick out each monster there is and fiesta gold begin just attacking the one the tank is killing.

You don't have to attack the one the tank is attacking though, but it is much recommended since you may or may not catch aggro. Catching aggro isn't necessarily a bad thing , but it can leave you a dead archer if you don't know what you are doing . Depending on where you are killing you probably shouldn't be dealing damage to other mobs unless it is your DoT skill . If you do catch aggro you want to promptly use nature's protection so as it will lose your attention quickly . If you are waiting for the cool down , since to a different monster and use hp stones as much as you can .

Don't worry if you do die . It is not the way to asking for a revive in the middle of an AoE . It will get you swiftly kicked from many parties . You want to wait until they have all the monsters under control . If your party has two clerics then you probably will either have a better chance of surviving or getting a revive in the middle of the attack .

AoE parties are something to gain large amounts of experience in a short amount of time. It takes a skilled archer to pull this off , but you can do it by simple taking the time to understand your archer and the party you are in .
