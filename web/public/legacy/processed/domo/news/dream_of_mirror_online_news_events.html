Dream of Mirror Online News & Events

Hello Dream of Mirror players ! 

This is just a little help for those that want to get started quickly in domo gold . read it and hope helpful for you ... 

All races begin the game as Citizen(Job/class) with the stats : STR 6 PHY 5 WIS 5 AGI 5 DEX 5 STA 5. You can change these stats until you reach beyond level 15 so i would advise just pooring your stats into STR for dmg.

First of all don't skip Newbie Village do ALL those quests by the time your out of there you will be level 5 and have armor, weapon and some pills and most important of all the Revival Point Portal Penny (teleport to set spawn point, 30min cooldown) which also can be bought for 1200G but thats quiet a lot for a newbie in domo gold.

Newbie Village Quest Item/Gold Rewards:
Cloth Cap, Gloves, Tunic, Pants, Slipper's, Beginner's Dagger, 20 x vitamin A pills,
5 x Level 10 Monster-Trapping Mirror, 300G.

When you teleport from Newbie Village you will arrive at Eversun City, talk first with the Eversun Guide which has a great quest which gives 10k XP just for talking to some NPCs . QUEST: Eversun Guided Tour

Also you can collect bones from wild pupu(level8), forest kuku papa(level9), domo gold bad baby bow-wow(level 10) all give 100 XP/bone and you have to hand in atleast 20 they stack at 100 items. Everytime you hand them in you get XP and either 5 x vitamin A pill or a random weapon that is quiet a lot better than the one you got at Newbie Village.

When your close by the DOJO in Eversun City you should talk to the NPC Team Guide (166,187) located outside of the DOJO which will explain about team (party) functions and give you the very useful titles "Team Member Wanted" and "Please Add Me To Your Team".

After completing Eversun Guidede Tour you should be level 6 now either exit out of the city from south or north. Running stright forward you will see baby bow-wow's i would advise you start with these since they drop hemp rope and Wild Dog Gang Badge which both are quest items and have a nice market price if you get some spares domo gold. 

After reaching level 7 you can choose to continue killing baby bow-wows or head out further and you will reach the Wild Pupu's they drop pupu bone which are each worth 100XP when handed into the Eversun Guide you will need atleast 20 (i think they stack at 100 if your worried about space).

At level 8 i would advise killing Wild Pupu unless there is a lot of people around and you have a hard time getting kills i would not advise moving on to forest kuku papa they are aggro and it is alittle bit slower XP plus you will need to use vitamin a pills if you have a STR build you can stay here killing Wild Pupu's until you reach level 10.

ONLY attributes(stat points) can be resetted upto and including level 15 for Citizen for other jobs you can also reset the skill points.

After you have completed qualification you get the chooice to be teleported to the DOJO where you can complete the job change.

First time you speak to the carreer advisor you will recive 2 x Voucher for Level 10 Equipment , buy domo gold which can be used to trade for a complete level 10 set of armors and weapon for your choosen job.
