Full Review: Dream of Mirror Online

Dream Of Mirror Online is a very well put together MMORPG. It’s beautiful anime graphics combined with a compelling story makes a great combination. 

When creating your character, you will have a plethora of options for making your character look just how you want. Remember, your choices really will determine your outcome. It is important to choose one of the four races that is going to benefit the class you choose later. When you reach level ten with domo gold, you will be able to choose one of thirteen impressive classes.

One of the strongest and most fun qualities in this game is the partner system. It is a linking system the encourages and rewards players that work together.

The combat in Dream of Mirror is fluid, and the experience grind is about the same as any other MMO. However, there is a fun combo system that dampens the blow of grinding. Overall, this game is very appealing to anime fans.
The Good: Many classes to choose from. A good sized community.

The Bad: A long journey of grinding. Non compelling quests.
