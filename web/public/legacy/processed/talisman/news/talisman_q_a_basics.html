Talisman Q/A: Basics

Suppose that you are sitting in your Demense and have this nice vessel in Talisman, then you need to build things. Please follow us and let us go into the different things you can build. Have a look please.

1) Ancient Tree of Life
This tree determines the level of your guild. Level 0 means you don't have one, and you won't be able to reach level(Talisman Gold) 2 unless you get this tree to level 2. Building this takes 76 hours or exactly 3 days so this is something you can't wait on.

2) The Statue of Sword
This determines what quests you have available to help your guild level. Each time you upgrade this you will get a new quest. If your guild is level 3 so we only have Rattan Cave, Snake Cave, and Mercy Temple. If you grow this ahead of your Ancient Tree, you will not get the new quest until your Ancient Tree is finished upgrading. So it is important to grow both. So far the prosperity to grow both of them has been identical so it is a matter of waiting for contruction to complete.
