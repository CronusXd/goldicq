DC Universe Online's Eye-catching Power Girl Cosplay

Power Girl, also known as Kara Zor-L or Karen Star is a DC Comics created super heroine. If you’re thinking to yourself that Power Girl is only a comic book character, then you’re sorely mistaken. This is VG Cosplay, so you know she’s appeared in the world of gaming. Most recently she has popped up in DC Universe Online from Sony for the Sony Playstation 3 and Microsoft Windows PC.

So how did Power Girl come to be? She’s the Earth-Two counterpart of Supergirl and the first cousin of Kal-L, aka Superman of the pre-Crisis Earth-Two. While a baby, Power Girl's parents enabled her to escape the destruction of Krypton. Although she left the planet at the same time that Superman did, her ship took much longer to reach Earth-Two.

Power Girl was at one time portrayed as having a highly athletic but slender physique. More recently she’s been given a more well-endowed shape. The character is consistently depicted as a large breasted young woman, (dcuo gold)and her physique is one of her most recognizable attributes to the extent that various writers have acknowledged it in both serious and humorous ways.
