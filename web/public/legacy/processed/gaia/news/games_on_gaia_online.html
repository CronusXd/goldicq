Games on Gaia Online

Gaia Online offers players 11 Flash games: Fishing, Towns, Word Bump, Jigsaw, Slots, Cinemas, Cards, Rally, Pinball and Electric Love Faktori. Users can win different items or gold. In Gaia Fishing, on the banks of various lakes, anything can be caught from different species of fish to garbage, which can be exchanged for fish-shaped hats or accessories. These items can also be sold on marketplace or back to the fishing store. The gold received is based on the rarity of the fish or item.

Towns
Towns is a virtual world where characters may interact with each other to collect gold and small items. Homes can be found throughout Towns. More than one user can visit other's home. Items which can be purchased from marketplace is used to design homes. Users also may use a chat system in the towns. gaia gold The towns often host events such as snowball fights and trick-or-treating.

Rally
Users can create a Scion with customizable wheels, decals, fog lights, tail lights and spoilers. Other car choices are the fictional Possum and Musculero. Users can display their cars and race with other users in an Adobe Flash game called Rally. Users are able to drive their cars on a simple track when racing.

Cinemas
Users are allowed to sit in a theater, and watch TV shows, cartoons and so on with other users. Users also have the ability to throw temporary items such as tomatoes, fireballs as well as other things during a cinema session. Quest movies also take place in cinemas, when a user finishes watching a trailer for a movie where they may receive a quest item. New films are released in gaia gold Cinemas every Monday.

Arena
Four main arenas: Housing, Art, Avatar and Writing are on Gaia Online. Members can vote in arenas and comment on art, avatars, houses or written works.
