Scarlet Legacy OBT Review


Crafting System

Scarlet Legacy has a somewhat interesting crafting system, and the multiple professions are based on three main ones known as mineral hunting, herb collecting, and animal hunting. From these three stem the professions of weapon making, armor making, cuisine cooking, medicine making, aura extract, and treasure making. The crafting in this game essentially involves afking for long periods of time while gathering materials and then using them to level up the various crafting professions to create some nice items.

Mounts/Fashions

This game definitely has some of the most unique and cool mounts I've seen out of any mmo. I would recommend checking out the video that was made(not by me). Here's a picture of me and my zombie mount that I won from a closed beta event. There are also a few fashions so far and im sure many more will be released in the item mall which is coming September 1st.

Pet System

The pet system is somewhat interesting. Each pet has stats and a specific "God Talent", which gives bonuses to the pet or player. You can also combine pets of the same type to create a better pet. An interesting part of the pet system is that when pets reach a certain level, you gain gift points which can be used to enhance the pets or your character.

Skill System

The skill system of this game is somewhat generic, but it gets the job done. Every few levels you can increase the levels of skills you've already learned and learn new skills. There is also a talent point system, which allows you to put points in things that enhance specific skills that your character has. There are multiple paths on the talent map and there is room for many unique builds.

Community

The community in this game is truly awesome and thats always a huuuge plus to me whenever I play a game. The people I've met so far have been awesome and as with any other game, you can join a guild, which I highly recommend doing. You get rewards for partying with people on your friends list and you can even get a weekly salary from your guild depending on how much you've contributed that week. Another great part of the community in this game are the very active GMs. I see them in-game a lot and they are always very helpful whenever someone needs something, including on the forums. They have also been hosting weekend events so far and those are always great in game.

Overall

This is a great game overall so far and although it seems like a generic asian mmo, it has a lot of cool features and I have no doubt that it will get even better as time goes on. This game really hits home for me and I always enjoy playing it.
