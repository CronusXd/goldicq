Scarlet Legacy Revealed By GameCampus

GameCampus, a publisher in North America, revealed a new Chinese MMO action game Scarlet Legacy. Scarlet Legacy, which is developed by a Taiwanese studio InterServ, will start its Closed Beta in China server. Players can land on the official site to apply for Closed Beta Test.

Only four classes set for this game, they are Warrior, Assassin, Monk and Mystic. The task that they have to fulfill is to save the princess from the evil. A new automatic support system will be applied in the game, that is, auto pilot-fighting allows for simplified play and convenience to players by equipping potions and pre-set buffs and skills to the player's class. What's more, the unique Chain Kill System will improve the grinding process allowing players to gain special rewards for over 100 or 1K enemies killed!
