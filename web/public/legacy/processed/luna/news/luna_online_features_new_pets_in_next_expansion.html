Luna Online Features New Pets in Next Expansion

The first massive expansion for Luna Online is named Luna Plus and will bring lots of new features to the game. From next January 19th, all its players will enjoy 60 combat pets, a cool feature, as everyone loves pets, right?

Have a look at some of them, after the jump.

In addition to all the new pets, the Pet System is going to be changed. When Luna Plus launches, all players will have the chance to level up their pets with their characters, get pet-specific gear and new skills. Each combat pet will have unique skills and a special “signature skill”. Pets are going to be better in the upcoming expansion.

From the adorable pink Harpy Jr which can silence all enemies in a player’s surroundings or the stealth Assassin that offers a cloak of invisibility until the battle starts, these combat pets are certainly more than meets the eye! The pretty Butterfly packs a punch when it gives an increase in all magical damage and defense to all friendly targets in a player’s perimeter. The Mantis has no need for praying, as it punctures enemies with a piercing strike, lowering the physical defense of targets. Indestructible, the Haunted Amor is fierce in its resilience and protects its master by drawing all aggressive targets in a player’s area.

Now you know Luna Online Gold brings new pets in next expansion, have you thought which one would you like to take along with you?
