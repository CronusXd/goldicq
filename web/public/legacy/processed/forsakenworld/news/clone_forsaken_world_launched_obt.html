Clone Forsaken World Launched OBT

Forsaken World launched OBT?I happened to find out this news headline while browsing today's game news. I didn't know that it was a clone of Forsaken World until I looked through the news. What on earth the game is?

This game is called Shen Mo Zhuan which is very similar to the Chinese name of Forsaken World: Shen Mo Da Lu.

The game offers two basic factions: the Forsaken World Gold and the Devil and the two factions can stage fierce wars against each other.

In addition to popular controls with the WASD keys and the mouse, the game also provides an auto-trace function so that players will not get lost. In the game, players won't have to dedicate to slaying monsters. With the help of the offline training function, they can level up easily. The most important thing is that the game will be free forever.

In Shen Mo Zhuan, Players can create their guild base and upgrade it through completing guild quests. Besides, guilds are allowed to fight against one another for the opponent's territory, arousing a sense of honor in the guild members.

About Shen Mo Zhuan:

  1. Its name resembles Forsaken World's Chinese name.
  2. Its concept art and logo are very similar to Forsaken World's.
  3. The game content is simple and boring without any innovative gameplay.
  4. It is a free game, but it sells items for offline leveling-up.
  5. It has an exaggerative slogan, but dare not release screenshots.

Games should be full of creativity and enthusiasm, we don't need any clones. So how long will Shen Mo Zhuan exist? And how many players will it win?
