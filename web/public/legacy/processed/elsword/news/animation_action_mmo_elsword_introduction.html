Animation Action MMO Elsword Introduction

Developed by KOG, Elsword is a new cartoon-style Action MMO! Elsword was alread published in HK and TW by Gamania and it will come to Europe soon! 

In Elsword, each class has its representative and unique combat action which will take on splendid and cool game screens when being wielded. Different classes can also use a same key to assume different attack modes so as to meet the enemies. Gamers who are used to playing large-scale game stations can even use an external joystick directly and step on the road to being a game king!

More features: Interactive Comic, Easy Control, Speed Action, Classic RPG Elements.
