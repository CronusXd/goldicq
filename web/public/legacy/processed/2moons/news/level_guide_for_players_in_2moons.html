Level guide for players in 2moons

2moons:As for skills, you will want to max all your dagger skills. We know many said that Voilent Step wasn't worth the skill points but honestly, we loved using it in pvp as an extra escape move incase Dead Spin was on cooldown, plus it does nice damage once maxed. Your going to restat/reskill at level 90 with this build anyway, and you'll always have skill points left over. You'll also want to max your Wind Moving, Cure Wounds, and Terrace Magic once you get those. Now 1 thing we discovered is the use of 2-3 xbow skills that went greatly with this dagger build. 


You will only be able to use a level 7 xbow with this build, so just get the best 1 you can find +3 it and put in some Rhombus's for lower level, and once you get in your 70s or so you can switch out for 4-5% gems. The first skill we loved was Explosion Arrow, make sure you always level this up every 10 levels(2moons dil). Right after you Dead Spin someone in pvp, switch quickly to your xbow in your offhand and hit them hard with an Explosion Arrow, it will knock a huge chunk off them.


The other 2 were just for extra escapes and damage and to have more than the same 3 skills all the time. Get Wonder Firing and Crow Wings once you reach those levels, and level them up every chance. These skills are just extra insurance for the higher defensive classes that might do alot of knockback on you.
