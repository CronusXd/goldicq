The guide about leveling if you want to Level 60

Hey guys, now here is a guide for all the 2moons players. The guide explained PTs in the game. I think it will be helpful for you. Hope you will like it. 

Ok, by now you have reach level 60 which is a major level to reach in game because it opens the option for you to start joining Parties for Crespo A, B, and C Dungeon Runs. Now at this stage having level 5 Alde still is useful even with the nerf, because of his chance of still stunning the Boss at the end- most likely your not going to be getting that far in the low 60s, but later on it’s useful.

By now you also have your first AOE Pet being Tent, one of the best and useful 2moons dil to have for PvE and PvP. Now most of the time people do not like summoners using pets in Crespo Dungeon Runs because they are big and get in the way, SO when it comes down to AOEs you only have 3 really.

So again continue to pump Summon Ghost, Blade Spin, and Blood Bats. Blood Bats not only is dekaron dil to help kill the mobs, but at times the HP gained can save you. And Clearly Pump Devil Swing and Skin until they are Maxed.

Just because a player is blue to you, doesn’t necessarily mean that he’s well intentioned. Stay alert and always expect the unexpected. A healthy dose of paranoia can save you in uncertain situations. Some players may try to scam you or trap you. If a trade deal seems too good to be true, it probably is. Always be careful when trading with other players and make sure you have dekaron dil　and do it in a reasonably safe location. Always double-check the items in the trade window before you accept a trade to avoid being tricked.

What to Pump in Between Posted Skills: Continue to Pump Devil Swing, Skin of Foreign Relm, Tent, Summon Ghost, Blade Dance, and Blood Bats. Alternative: Again if you do not intend on reskilling alot, we would recommend leaving Blood Bats at Level 1 and save your skill points.
