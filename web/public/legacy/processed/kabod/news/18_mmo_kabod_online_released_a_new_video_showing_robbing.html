18+ MMO Kabod Online Released a New Video Showing Robbing

Kabod Online, an MMORPG being developed by Korea-based Kadod Entertainment, will meet all players with its first closed beta test. Recently, Kabod Online released a new gameplay video for its upcoming CBT.

The video unveiled this time is from the real gameplay of Kabod Online, showcasing the fast-paced game rhythm and vivid detail of the armor damage when players are fighting with monsters. In Kabod Online, characters and monsters will suffer equipment losses when being attacked. The damage to equipment falls into three degrees. When the equipment is damaged to the highest degree, (Kabod Gold)the character will be shown in an almost nude state. In addition, Kabod Online offers a "Criminal System" wherein players can PK anyone and loot their items.
