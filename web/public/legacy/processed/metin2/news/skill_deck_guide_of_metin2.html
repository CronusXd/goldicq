Skill Deck Guide Of Metin2

The rotating skill deck in Metin2 is a unique feature that allows you to decide the flow of combat before ever stepping into battle, keeping the key on action rather than flooding you with options while in the thick of it. Upon entering combat mode via a quick tap of the F key, the skill deck will appear at the bottom of your screen, presenting you with a short horizontal row of up to 5 skill options.

Executing your initial attack will then vertically roll the deck, presenting you with a second row of skills. Players start out with 2 rows of 3 skill slots each, but as you advance through Fame ranks, new columns and rows open up to help round out which tactics you want to employ. You'll eventually be able to tie together different types of attacks which grant increased benefits, so long as you successfully land each attack in the chain. Navigating the skill deck can be done in a couple of different ways, allowing you to search for a method that works best for your particular playstyle. Each horizontal row will own 1 to 5 skills which are designed to the number keys on your keyboard.

Pressing the number key for that particular row will 'arm' that row of attacks, or you can use the scroll wheel on your mouse to navigate horizontally across the row in either direction in turn. It is a good idea to get comfortable with whichever method works best for you early on. To use the skills themselves, metin2 yang hold down your RMB, think of this as 'arming' your skills for attack. Once the red indicator on your crosshair is lit up, a LMB click will then shoot atwhichever skill is currently active, suggested by a glowing boarder near it's icon.
