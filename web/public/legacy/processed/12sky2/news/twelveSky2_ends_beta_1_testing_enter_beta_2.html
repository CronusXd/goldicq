TwelveSky2: Ends Beta I Testing, Enter Beta II

MMORPG "TwelveSky 2" has closed Beta Phase I and is now ready to enter Beta Phase II. The successor to the already popular "TwelveSky" will officially be opening up enrollments today (March 10th) for the second phase of Closed Beta in order to give more players in the Western market a sneak preview at the first English version of the game. Meanwhile the fun will continue on March 23rd as many additional players will be able to pick up arms with their fellow gamers and wander through the mesmerizing towns of ancient China in search of great adventure and conquest.

A post on their website says the following: "At this time we would like to officially proclaim that Closed Beta Phase I is now over. As a result, game access will be suspended until Phase II launches on March 23rd. During this time, we encourage all members to be active participants on our official message board while the Live Operations team works closely with ALT1 in order to make your upcoming experience even greater. It was an honor to finally join you all, side-by-side, during this gigantic milestone, and we look forward to even greater success!"


MAYN Interactive Pte Ltd., TwelveSky 2's European publisher is also very happy so far with the progress that is being made. "Having officially concluded as of March 9th, Closed Beta Phase I was a great success!" Said Young Shin, CEO of MAYN. Now that the initial testing is complete, we're excited to bring this fantastic title to the next level."
