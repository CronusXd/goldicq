Cool things about 12Sky2

This thread will be devoted towards showing you, the users (mainly the one's curious about 12sky2 and possibly have only played 12sky1), some very cool features or things we think you may find highly interesting. 

I will be updating and maintaining this thread. If you have any requests about stuff, I'll do my best to answer you, and post the info on the OP. 

Faction Announcements will be sent as a notice to all faction members within all maps (including common maps). 

Assistant Faction Leader designation will be up to 12 Assistant Faction Leaders, which the qualifications for will be 1,000 CP and Master lvl1.

CashBox will be available for the FL to withdraw silver from upon the condition that more then 3 Assistant Faction Leaders have been desifnated.

Faction Weapons(Stone Thrower) can be purchased by the Faction Leader for 100mil silver per Weapon. The Faction Leader talks to the Faction's guardian and selects (Faction Weapon) to purchase. Assistant Faction Leaders can also purchase Faction Weapons.

Summoning Faction Weapons(Stone Throwers) can only be done on maps other then the central PvP valley and your own 12sky2 Gold. A maximum of 5 units can be placed for each map.

Faction Leader term is 1 month, and a new leader will be elected by voting after that.

Faction Leader Voting System:

Day1-5, Candidates nomination time, starts day1 00:00 and ends day5 23:59, candidates must be M1+ and have over 1000cp.

Day6-10, Faction Leader voting time, starts day6 00:00 and ends day 10 23:59, voters must be M1+ in order to vote for Faction Leader, and no CP requirement.

Day11, Faction Leader voting count, starts day11 00:00 ends day11 20:59,depending on the voter's level, depends how many voting points they get Twelve Sky2 Gold(ie: M1= 1 point, M33= 33 points).

Day11, Faction Leader announcement, day11 21:00, the new Faction Leader is announced to the faction and all Faction Leaders and Assistant Faction Leaders are removed instantly and the power of Faction Leader is transferred to the newly elected Faction Leaders.
