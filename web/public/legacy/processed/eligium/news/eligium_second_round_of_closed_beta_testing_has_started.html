Eligium: Second Round of Closed Beta Testing Has Started

The call has gone out from publishers Frogster to all of the warriors out there because the second round of Closed Beta testing of Eligium has begun. This is the final round of Beta testing for the upcoming MMORPG. 

Eligium The second and final round of Closed Beta testing has officially begun for the free-to-play MMORPG, Eligium. That means there are even more foes to be slain and glorious victory to be had. This round of the Closed Beta testing phase will end on January 18th, so those who applied may still have to don armor and heed the call to arms.

The first round of Beta testing in Eligium was apparently successful, as publishers Frogster have given thanks for all of the helpful feedback and improvement suggestions they received from the testers themselves.

This good mean good things for the future of the title and perhaps could mean that Eligium (Eligium Gold) may soon enter the Open Beta testing phase. Keep your eyes peeled for any new information that we come across!
