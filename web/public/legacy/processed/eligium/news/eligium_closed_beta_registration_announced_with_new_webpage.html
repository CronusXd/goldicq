Eligium: Closed Beta Registration Announced with New Webpage

Frogster Online Gaming has planned to only give out treats with the launch of their new website for the free-to-play MMORPG, Eligium. In addition to the new site, they have also kicked off a light competition to offer up Beta keys! 

Eligium A fancy new homepage for the free-to-play MMORPG, Eligium, has been launched by publishers Frogster Online Gaming (Runes of Magic, Throne of Fire). On the new homepage, you will be able to find plenty of information, lore, concept art and the forums. This gives you a chance to get more involved with the upcoming title and become a part of the community.

Although there is no date as to when the Closed Beta testing phase of Eligium (Eligium Gold) will begin, you still have a chance to register for it. Join up on the community forums and have a look at what you’ll have to do to participate… it is a bit different than your normal sign-ups!

Instead of simply registering and waiting for your chance to get a key, you have a chance to really wow the team of Eligium. To celebrate the season, they have asked that fans post their favorite/best pumpkin carvings in the forums. The top 25 will be selected and given a Beta key!
