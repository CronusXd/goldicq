Eligium: The Closed Beta Has Been Announced

Frogster Online Gaming, a games publisher in Germany, announced today that the Closed Beta testing phase of the upcoming free-to-play MMORPG, Eligium, is due to begin in the coming weeks and they have listed some instructions for potential testers. 

As we had already discussed in a previous article, the Closed Beta registration for Eligium has been open for some time now. However, Frogster Online Gaming has finally given fans a launch date for the free-to-play MMORPG!

If you haven’t done so already, you can head over to the Eligium homepage and register there. Once registered, (Eligium Gold)just wait and hope for to receive a Closed Beta key! That means you will get the chance to test out the online game before anyone else.

However, Frogster Online Gaming has stated that those characters who participate in this first round test will be deleted before the Open Beta tests starts.
