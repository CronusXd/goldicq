Ndoors Unveils New PvP System to WonderKing

WonderKing, the highly successful 2D side-scrolling massively multi-player online role-playing game (MMORPG) from Ndoors Interactive is having players try its revamped Player vs. Player (PvP) system. This new upgrade will keep players busy as they take part in one-on-one battles to secure WonderKing supremacy.

Features:

Immersive, in-depth user interface: Players will now be able to see statistics and other information about their opponent, including their internet latency rate, when going in for a fight.

All-new strategy-enhancing mechanics: Reminiscent of many beloved classic arcade fighting games,wonderking zed players will now take part in a three-round match. Each player can choose a map to play where they feel it to be advantageous to their character. This encouraging gamers to think on their feet and contrive ways to exploit their opponent’s weaknesses rather than relying on luck in a one-off match.

Dedicated battlefield: Players can now compete against friends in an arena dedicated to PvP matches. They can teleport to the arena from almost any map in WonderKing with ease. Talking to Non-Player Character (NPC) Shaina will initiate a challenge.

Increased rankings and titles for victors: Players will be rewarded with special titles as they move up the ranks by winning PvP battles. The more they win the more they get!
