WonderKing Enters Final Closed Beta with Fiesta Event

Ndoors Interactive, a leading publisher of online games world-wide, presents the fourth closed beta for its highly anticipated 2D side-scrolling massively multi-player online role-playing game (MMORPG) WonderKing. In honor of the last closed beta period, WonderKing welcomes players to its "Fiesta Event" where each community member will receive a special in-game gift from Ndoors. And, for the first twenty-four hours of the closed beta, the game will also give away whimsical Heart Face Tags for fans. As if that weren't enough, Ndoors will also be hosting special bonus experience events and monster drops in WonderKing throughout the closed beta period.

"WonderKing's closed beta testing has been a rewarding, insightful experience for us at Ndoors, and we are thrilled to reward the players who helped bring the game closer to open beta launch," said Peter Kang, CEO, Ndoors Interactive. "Our fans have been an immense help in getting us this far, and with their help we are putting the final touches on a new fun and exciting online game. As a 'thank you,' we are showering the players with wonderking zed, awesome giveaways, and much more during Closed Beta IV."

WonderKing is a vibrant, colorful 2D side-scrolling game that boasts delightfully animated graphics and a compelling story for players of all ages to enjoy. WonderKing is highlighted by a captivating story of heroism set in a unique fantasy world: Players battle a sinister dark force, the Sinistry of Demons, for the chance to be crowned the wonderking money. It is prophesied that only the WonderKing can save the world from darkness by defeating the evil Baalon, King of Demons, who has plunged the world into unrelenting turmoil.
