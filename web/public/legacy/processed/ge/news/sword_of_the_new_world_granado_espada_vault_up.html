Sword of The New World: Granado Espada Vault Up

Sword of The New World: Granado Espada is a new highly-anticipated full 3D MMORPG (Massive Multiplayer Online Role-playing game) from the creators of Ragnarok Online. It will be the first English version of the game and will cater to the following countries: Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Vietnam, Philippines, New Zealand and Brunei.Infocomm Asia Holdings Pte Ltd is the official licensee to operate Granado Espada in the above mentioned regions.

Granado Espada developer HanbitSoft confirmed in a report that they will use a new 3D game engine to develop the sequel to Granado Espada. We've translated and summarized the report as follows for you:
