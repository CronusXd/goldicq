Black Prophecy: New Sci-Fi Real Time Action MMO

With stressing the word "Real Time Action", a new sci-fi MMO which entitled Black Prophecy comes to us. We can easily find that the gameplay of Black Prophecy is a bit furious. The developer Reakktor Media (makers of NEOCRON) haven`t announced the game`s release date. We just got some features and screenshots about Black Prophecy. Hope you enjoy it. 

Indicidually mofidy and optimise your ship with a vast range of modules,parts and weapons which offer a tremendous numberof possible combinations to perfectly match your style of play.

Spectacular Audiovisual Presentation

Utilising state-of-the-art graphic technology and a cinemctic orchestra soundtrack.black Prophecy creats a captivating atmosphere unknown to any other MMO.

Play by yourself, in a clan or together with a whole fleet to subdue your opponents and uncover secrets of cosmic proportions. Victory and defeat of every battle affects the well-being of your faction and influences the whole game world. Capture precious resources to improve your ship, your space station and the power of your clan.

In the 26th century,mankind has expanded across the galaxy,yet only few of its colonies are controlled by Humans.The true rulers are the cybernetically enhanced Tyi and the biogenetically perfected Genides.Their striving for total control over all human species drives them into the territory of an accient alien race that does not tolerate other cosmic civilisations.
To survive this war and uncover the motives of the alien`s destructive behaviour.you are forced to join one of the two dominant human races.Their search for the origin of the Restorers becomes a race for survivals of all three human species.

Created in close cooperation with science fiction writer Michael Marrak,(Black Prophecy Gold)the online universe of Black Prophecy lets players jump into countless missions and massive cinematic space battles,to save and sculpt the future of mankind.
