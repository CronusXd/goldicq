Archlord: Tips on Sorcerer Build

Well hello everybody! Here are some tips on Sorcerer build for all the Archlord players. If you have any trouble in this portion, just go through and look for usefull information.

1. Always do pvp or pve guide dont do hybrid cos there are too many variations with a lot of people having CC and/or unique accs

2. Always max out essential buffs 1st (no point having strong attacks if ur not alive to use them)

3. Lvl 1 - 40 always have armor stones and as much defense as poss(archlord gold)

4. 40 + u want stamina stamina stamina thats more important than most
so for example a 4 slot armor top would look like this --> Hp+ / Hp% / Phys resist / then last slot is a choice armor stone or stun resist stone

5. Weapons - its dependent on class and lvl but 1 - 56 (some cases 60+)
ALWAYS have the same element NEVER mix them i.e 1 of each element is a no no.. now some 60+ will have mix of ONE type of element and attack stones.. i.e 8 slot weap --> 4 lvl 3 attack stones at top and 4 lvl 5 sea stones at bottom..

6. ALWAYS stone attack before the element.archlord gold  REASON --> upon failure attack stones can reset all the stones u previously put in.. i.e u succsesfully upgrade 4 sea stones and 3 attack stones then last attack stone can and has failed and resets all back to empty 8 slot..

7. Have fun learn your character, dont beg in game (its annoying)and dont be a carebear and fight for your grinding spot.
