Demonic turkeys and new content invade Kitsu Saga

Kitsu Saga, Aeria Game's free-to-play MMORPG featuring the chibi-fox spirits known as Kitsu, has received a new major update.

The update adds a limited time event, in which demonic turkeys and festively-dressed Kitsu invade the lands in the spirit of the upcoming Thanksgiving holiday, and will grant limited edition Thanksgiving items as rewards.

The update also adds the following content:

Level Cap increased to 60

A new tier of mastery Skills unlocked(Kitsu Saga Gold)

4 New Zones:
Bamboo Ridge (Lv46-50)
Eversun Ford (Lv46-50)
Grassgreen County (Lv50-55)
Rose Cloud Mountain (Lv55-60)
5 New Dungeons:
Lethal Mountain Dungeon (Lv. 50 Instance 2 man dungeon)
Sunny Canal Dungeon (Lv. 50 Instance 5 man dungeon)
Bloody Flame Citadel (Lv. 60 Instance 5 man dungeon)
Burning Iron Claw City(Lv. 60 5 man Instance dungeon)
Cursed 7 Star Temple (Lv. 50 10 man raid dungeon) - Note: previous post was incorrect
The party leader can now convert a party into a raid party. This will allow up to 10 people in a party, however you will not gain any exp or loot outside of a raid dungeon.

Imperial City Emergency (Sieged Purplecloud Temple)
- Special acitivity with special rewards!
