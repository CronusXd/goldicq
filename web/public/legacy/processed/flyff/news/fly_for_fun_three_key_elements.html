Fly For Fun - Three Key Elements

Created to watch over the world of Roika, the goddess Rhisis did so for a time. However, she felt alone, and thus decided to fashion three other beings, each containing aspects of herself. Bubble was her kindness and love, and Iblis her apathy. Shade, the embodiment of her hatred and fear, was the one who set out to destroy the land and its inhabitants, making a species called Masquerpets to carry out her will. Those humans who survived their initial attacks knew only too well that they would have to prepare and arm themselves for even greater threats to come.

Fly For Fun, often called flyff penya, is Aeonsoft's massively multiplayer release based on this scenario. Offered in North America through Gala-Net's portal, gPotato, the colorful title naturally features the ability to fly. It also has various continents to explore, from Flaris with its underground caverns to the lava flows of perilous Darkon. and the Garden of Rhisis, now just a lifeless shadow of its former glory, although there are some who believe Iblis still returns there to pine. Other notable elements include a broad assortment of monsters to fight, and a character system that allows progression into eight final sub-classes.
