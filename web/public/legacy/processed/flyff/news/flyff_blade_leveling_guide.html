Flyff blade leveling guide

This is a blade leveling guide.This section is for people who'll be using an AoE build from the start.If you have just started playing a blade in Fly for Fun,then this is the portion you should read.

15-45
Unless you have another character to fund your AoE blade, getting to 70 with only 15 STR is going to be a major annoyance. You can either tough it out, or invest in some demols and vigor rings. Having assist friends who don’t mind buffing you occasionally can be helpful as well.

At level 25, you’ll be able to take advantage of the skills Smite Axe and Blazing Sword. Use these to make up for your low attack rate. When maxed, they don’t add that much compared to the level 30 masteries, but they’re useful nonetheless. At level 30, you can unlock Axe Mastery and Sword Mastery, which are better than the previous skills. Choose to use either swords or axes, and only max the corresponding set of masteries. Later on, you’ll have enough SP to max both, but it’s better to max one set right away for optimum damage, instead of trying to get them both to high levels.

45-70
If you can afford a Panggril/Stitch set, things will become a lot easier at 45. With all the pieces, this set gives the following bonuses:
FP Consume -10%, Def+45, HP +10% and ASPD +20%
You may not be dealing out much damage, but the fact that you’re hitting a lot faster does help.

At 60, you can stick to the 45 set, or switch to Rescorn/Recorn. The complete set gives the following bonuses:
HP +15%, Def +50, ASPD +20%.
This is a great 1v1 levelling set, and the def. and HP bonuses are excellent for AoEing as well.

Note: Don't forget about quests! In addition to the office quests, try to complete these helpful pre-70 quests for some fairly easy exp:
Red Bangs - obtainable at level 30 from Luda in flyff penya.
Disappeared Play Script - obtainable at level 37 from Ruminerr in Saintmorning.
Ivillis Temple - obtainable at level 45 from the Mayor of Saintmorning.
Red Bangs Part 2 - obtainable at level 60 from Luda in Flarine.
Legendary Red Mantis - obtainable at level 60 from Rudvihil in Flarine.
