Free Realms Announces Hoverboards

Free Realms has announced the arrival of Hoverboards to the game! Hoverboards, made popular back in 1989 in a tiny little movie, will allow players to get around quicker and in a much more stylish fashion than hoofin' it! The boards will also allow players to move at 150 percent of their character's normal walking speed and show off with board trick animations. The Precursor Tech Hoverboard is currently available in a brown and blue version and a brown and orange version, each for 550 Station Cash in the Marketplace. 

Also available in Free Realms today are Fall Away Blocks, Fluffy and Storm Cloud Blocks and small, medium and large Bounce Pads. These new items are perfect accessories for player imagination and building on the recently introduced Vacant Lots in Snowhill and Briarwood. From tree houses and obstacle courses to mazes in the sky, (Free Realms Coins)Vacant Lots are empty plots of land ripe for constructing anything players can dream up.
