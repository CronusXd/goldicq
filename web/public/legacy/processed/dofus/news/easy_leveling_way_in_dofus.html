Easy Leveling way In Dofus

Following there are some information for level 12-25 in Dofus. Have a look as that can help you a lot to play this game.

Levels 12-18
At these levels you should keep fighting chafers if you stay in ircanam long enogh. If you go to astrub try quests that give alot of xp and try crackrock with higher level friends. Try dungons with alot of friends and increase your wisdom with the cawwot dofus, prespic headgear and cloak and many more. Make money with jobs and equipment selling.

Levels(dofus kamas) 19-25
Do more dungons and use scrolls that give you better stats and learn elementary spells or sell them to get money. Learn special spells and battle more crackrocks. Lastly, use stratagys.

Perhaps many of the players have some better ways, this is not absolute, and the way we provide for you only reference. You should find your own way of easy leveling and acheter des kamas making and use it to become level200. Hope you can have a good time with this game.

Spells:
Fortune and Reducing key: to level 6 if not already.
Weapon Skill / Pandoras Box: This slot is up to you, you may choose to level one of the spells mentioned earlier if you like it. Just remember to save some for level 6 Coins Throwing.
Soul Capture: its always a good idea.

Equipment:
Levels(dofus kamas) 101-110: Dark Miners hat, Dragon Pig Cape, Dragon Pig Ring.
Levels 110-120: Ancestral Set, Shovel Ijah
Levels 120-130: Loads of new equipment here, Sandal Titude, Dreggon Belt, Metal Hammer, Mastralis Croak, Doro L.
