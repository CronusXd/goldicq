Bellato Warrior Classes in RF

Here is a guide on the bellato warrior class of Cabal,and it's not my work.I took it from other website,which edited by sunshine.Hope you could enjoy the game.In RF,they have the highest defense, and that doubles with a shield along with block rate. And yes, blocks work against magic. They can do great damage with two handed weapons, but then they are within range of alot of people so they can die quickly that way. This is why most will go Sword/Shield.

Along with going RF Sword/Shield many will use ranger armor. This basically gives more attack speed, faster movement, and resists. These resists also counter the -20% all elements that shield gives and the lowering defense using defense talics(in a new update each +defense talic lowers all resists some). While this means more training to raise your ranged PT, it is worth it if you want to be strong.

Now at level 30 you have two choices, either offensive as Commando or Defensive as Miller.

- Commando has a skilled called Howl to buff your melee damage. However, if you look at Cora theirs buffs both offense and RF Online Gold defense and Accretia have higher armor than the others, along with being able to get a damage buff later on. Though, Commando will have access to Holy magic so it's not bad in comparison.

- The Miller is an amazing tank for PvE. It's the best sheild wielding tank in the game when you go to sheild Miller because of the defensive shield buffs and holy magic. However it has absolutely no offensive skills except a life leaching one which is more to help you heal yourself.

After you have to choose one class between Berserker, Armsman, Shield Miller, depending on what you picked. (You usually have 2 choices, just excluding specialst that only has 1 choice. There is one exclusive to the choice and one shared, usually. Note the graphic)

- Berserker gets a special attack that deals more damage than some other attack skills, and a slowing one. This slow is simular to the "spin-a-web" force spell that anyone with tier2 magic can get, however, so I don't find it overly useful.

- Armsman gets an AoE attack that'll only be much use in PvE. The only use I can see in it is leveling faster. Also the Armsman gets a skill that lowers the enemies stamina and stamina recovery. How useful is this? Well, how useful is just hitting someone one time? Answere: not very since they can RF Online Gold recover with a potion. (For you that don't already know, when you're out of stamina you can only walk)


- Lastly is the Shield Miller. This class is great at taking damage, however not at dishing it. Both skills the Shield Miller have are good. One further increases defense and the other drains the targets HP and heals you in return. Now, how useful is that you wonder? Considering at higher levels you have much more HP than potions heal, and that potions have a 3 second timer, it IS good. Note that with Shield Miller you must use a shield for those 2 great buffs.

You might want to go Commando/Miller dual class or vice versa with Bellato's Warrior, then you have both an attack buff and defensive buff. It's not better but not worse except you might have less hp.Don't think that you won't do any damage in pvp with a shield Miller. The less damage you do isn't nearly as much as the extra damage you can potentially take if 20 people are attacking you.
