Troy Online Announces Official Launch

ALT1Games has announced Troy Online 's official service schedule. Troy Online is currently in the open beta testing phase now and will end at 10 p.m. (PST) on August 18th. ALT1Games will start the official services at 11 a.m. (PST) on August 19th.

Also ALT1Games announced that the game registration conditions will change in the upcoming commercial release so that users can log on to Troy Online and play the game without requiring a full membership. The new registration conditions were implemented to allow users to play Troy Online Gold by just registering on the homepage without e-mail verification. With the new log in system in place, ALT1Games wishes to grant more users the opportunity to experience Troy Online.

"During the open beta test, ALT1Games has encountered some issues such as server connection difficulties and lagging which mainly occurred when too many users tried to connect to the servers at the same time," commented Alex Chang, Troy's Business Development Manager, "These problems have already been addressed and will be fixed before Troy Online Gold ’s official commercial release. Also there will be many events in place to celebrate the launching of Troy Online’s commercial release tomorrow!"

Its uncommon to see a company go to such lengths to change their game just before launch and OnRPG is excited to see how much the game has improved with this official launch.
