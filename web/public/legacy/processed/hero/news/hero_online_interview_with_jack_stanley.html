Hero Online Interview With Jack Stanley

Mgame?s new title Hero Online, a full 3D martial arts MMORPG is set to start its closed beta in North America on 15th June, 2006.Today, Jack Stanley,the leader designer of Hero Online,come to tell us this game.

MMOsite.com: The start of closed beta test indicates that the first phase development for Hero Online is winding down. Can you have a brief review of the development process of Hero Online in the past few years?
Jack Stanley: When I was youngster, I dressed up and pretended with my friends that I was a ninja out to fight the injustices of the world. I imagined that I had the ability to disappear on a whim with the ability to run alongside alls and jump super-high distances. With Hero Online, I can do all of these without embarrassing myself!
We have started the development of Hero Online, in the year of 2002. At that time, the genre Martial Art Action MMORPG was not well known and nor was it popular, so many developers were looking for developing this type of game to attract the players who always seek for something new. With knowing this fact, we have tried to set a difference against other developers and the main stresses that we have put in order to differentiate our game from others are game storyline, pet system, skill system and warfare system and these assets of hero online gold has kept its uniqueness among many Martial Art Action MMORPG and generated a massive success in Korea.

MMOsite.com: Can you tell us how you came up with such a vast storyline?
Jack Stanley: The premise of the game is deeply interwoven with the storyline of the game, which can be found on our website. However, to put it simply, a long time ago, a group known as The Twelve Demons ravaged the lands in search of a rare and mystical treasure. They were defeated by a mysterious hero who killed them by luring them in to a trap. Twenty years after his great victory, the hero is mysteriously murdered and the thread of the Twelve Demons looms again. For the safety of the land and its people, the mystery of the Hero's murder must be solved and the threat of the Twelve Demons defeated. However, to do these things, a new Hero must be born.
