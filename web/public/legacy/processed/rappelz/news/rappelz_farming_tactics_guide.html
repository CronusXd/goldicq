Rappelz farming tactics guide

This is the farming tactics guide.If you have just started playing Rappelz,then this is the portion you should read.Hope it's useful for you.


a)Alright I can't stress this enough but stay in around the same place! Farming all over and moving to different areas will increase downtime,find a place where mobs spawn fast and in a good number.


b)Take the angro's first-There are a big group of monsters that automatically attack you even if you dont use heals, or attack it. If your target is near such a monster take the other one first as it will save you some HP and therefore save you downtime.


c)Have something to heal you- clerics, hunas and the devas have no problems.when I tried to farm with my SpS I had to run around finding anyone that could heal so I could get back to farming.


d)Try to clear out useless things in your inventory before you go farm. If you are mass farming, there will be a crapload of stuff comin in and reducing your moving speed slows you down a crapload.


e)Alright one, this helps you count your kills if you wanna count your kills. Second of all, it increases your killing speed by x2. You could get a pet in 1 day rather than 2 days if you do this. Don't worry, lak isnt a problem,tons of lak come from farming mobs.


f)Pickup every drop you see-Farming doesnt just bring pet cards, at one point I made more money with the cards and junk and money i found when farming than selling my BP card. 100 Rappelz Rupees each kill isnt alot, but farming 2000 mobs for a pet card while each dropping 100 rupee.


g)Patience-Yes the most important, you lose this and you'll never get a card. Stay till the very end.


h)There is such thing as luck but always assume its not on your side.


i)One more thing- People say luck affects drop rate and I have to say its BOGUS! I may be wrong but neither of these theories is proven. For the time being use str or vit rings instead of luck rings.
