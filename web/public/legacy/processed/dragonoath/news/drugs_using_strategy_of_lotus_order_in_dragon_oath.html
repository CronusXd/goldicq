Drugs Using Strategy of Lotus Order in Dragon Oath

Sometimes in the field of personal pk or pk, a small difference between can cause the wholesale restructuring. drugs are divided into three categories: is the pharmacy and send one of the general spirit of skills, which are cooking skills. Here we are talking about drugs inDragon Oath is not a PK refers to those drugs increase the blood back to blue, but BT drugs that can resist paralysis, bulk power, increased stamina, strength, shenfa, property attacks and can replace stone in PK.

First introduce the skills of pharmaceutical that anyone can learn. When achieve level (Dragon Oath Gold) 30 can go to find the pharmaceutical skills in Luoyang 138,164 or Dali 100,128 Fu Master Jing-Ming Zhang Angelica learning skills. You can find Master Bai Yingming pharmacology to learn the skills , and Pharmacology is to assist the pharmaceutical skills, can reduce the energy consumption of pharmaceuticals.After learn skills, you can learn in primary drug formulations at level 1-3, level 4-6 can study intermediate drug formulation,level (Dragon Oath Gold) 7-10 can learn advanced drug formulation.
