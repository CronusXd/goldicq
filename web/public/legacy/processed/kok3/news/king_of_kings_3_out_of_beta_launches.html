King of Kings 3 Out of Beta & Launches

This is the moment online gamers have been waiting for. The publisher gamigo has a special new treat to offer the role-playing game community: the clan warfare MMORPG King of Kings 3 is opening its gates to players in North America and Europe.

The feedback received from the community at the end of closed beta testing was overwhelming.

Thousands of players used the chance to participate actively in the development of King of Kings 3. For three months suggestions for improvement were collected and then implemented. Among other things, the fighting style and balancing were adapted. The game’s localization was also continually improved upon during closed beta testing. With the end of beta testing, players now have access to 15 new regions and characters can be leveled up to level 90. As a result, PvP combat has become even more dynamic.

Players especially enjoy the large number of features that give the game an incredible depth. The guild system stands out in particular because it allows small communities to band together to form a mighty kingdom with its very own guild town. Other highlights are the multi-tiered character classes, (King of Kings 3 Gold)the variety of minigames, dozens of mounts and character housing.

More information about King of Kings 3 can be found in the game’s official website.

Features at a glance
. Brilliantly-designed fantasy world full of variety
. Comprehensive PvE content with loads of quests and minigames
. Epic RvR warfare and intense PvP combat
. Multi-leveled guild system with guilds, legions, and kingdoms
. Unique housing and town building system
. Dozens of mounts that can also level up
. A total of 27 different character classes
