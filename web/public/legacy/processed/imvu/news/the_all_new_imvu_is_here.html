The All-New IMVU is Here!

We gave you a taste of the next-generation IMVU 3D Chat a couple of weeks ago.

Now you can check it out for yourself! Simply sign in to IMVU Credits 3D Chat and select “Update Now” and that’s it (or download here). Start enjoying the new IMVU experience now.

The future of your virtual self is here!
Shop right from 3D Chat. No more going to the website to buy your next outfit!

Change your outfit seamlessly and see it right on your 3D Chat Home Screen!
People Search now lives right in 3D Chat. Use the filters to narrow your search of IMVU Credits s 35 million avatars.

Click on Chat Rooms for an easy-to-search list of 3D Public Rooms.
Click here to get the full scoop on the All-New IMVU Credits.

FAQs
You can also find answers to many of the questions we’ve seen about the new IMVU Credits 3D Chat by reading the Frequently Asked Questions page here.

Tell us what you think!
The team at IMVU Credits has been working very hard on the new 3D Chat, and we hope you enjoy it as much as we do.

We want to hear that you think. If you have suggestions or experience an issue, we want to hear about it. You can share by commenting below or by clicking the “send feedback” link below the main menu on the 3D Chat Home Screen.

We’ll continue to add new features and functionality, as well as potentially make changes based on your feedback, so stay tuned!

Can I still use the old version of IMVU 3D Chat?
As a reminder, you’ll have the ability to toggle back and forth between the two versions by simply changing a setting in the Friends window > under the Settings menus > check or un-check “Use New UI”. Once you change that setting, you’ll need to restart IMVU Credits.

Additionally, if you find that you experience any difficulty with the new release, you’ll have the ability to go back to an older release if you choose to do so.

Please note that when you move from the old version of 3D Chat to the All-New Chat, similar to when you download the latest version today, your favorites are not carried over.

The good news is that with the new 3D Chat, we’ve made a change in how we store this information.

So now, Recently Visited and Favorite Chat Rooms will always be saved when you download more recent versions.
