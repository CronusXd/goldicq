The Frozen Prison is a dungeon with monsters ranging from levels 40 ~ 44, and the more difficult Sanctuary of Frozen Time a dungeon with monsters level 50 ~ 54.

The Frozen Prison and the Sanctuary of Frozen Time are ancient networks of tunnels far beneath the earth that have been fortified by the denizens that have made them their strongholds. Bundle up, and get ready to venture deep within the icy caverns that are home to some of the most despicable practitioners of dark magic!

With the war between the Olympian Gods and Titans raging throughout the world, thousands of warriors on both sides fall in battle daily. With Hades, the Lord of the Underworld, participating in the fight against the Titans two of the most powerful Necromancers in the world have taken his distraction as an opportunity to gain power for themselves(Legend of Edda gold).

Deidamia (pictured above) and her sister Inevis (another Elite Boss) have been harvesting the souls of those who fall in the conflict, harvesting them through occult rituals before the souls are able to finish their intended journey to the Underworld; and are using the power of those spirits to create an army of the undead.

The Bloody Anas'mas are scavengers that feed on the corpses left behind in the wake of Deidamia and Inevis' quest for power. In large numbers their hunger should not be underestimated by the living.

Barbaric savages from the frozen wastes of the North, the Horn Tiger Slaver tribes have been drawn to the sisters' call. With their stealthy Horn Tigers captured in the wild and bound to their command, they roam the caverns of The Frozen Prison and The Sanctuary of Frozen time. These voracious creatures serve as the scouts for Deidamia and Inevis, and are eager to hunt down and devour anyone foolish enough to venture into the stisters' lairs.

The Dark Ice Witches of the Frozen Prison and the Destroying Ice Witches (loe gold) of the Sanctuary of Frozen Time are acolytes of the sisters, trading their servitude and very souls for training in the dark arts. Specializing in Ice Magic, these witches will fight to the death to protect the hidden entrances to the lower levels of the caverns.

The Corrupted Ice Witches of the Frozen Prison and Mad Ice Witches (pictured above) of the Sanctuary of Frozen time are some of the more dangerous of Deidamia and Inevis' necromantic allies. Combining both Shadow and Ice magic, they are even more deadly and fanatic than their acolyte sisters and will stop at nothing in their quest to share in the sisters' newfound dark power.
