Dragon Ball Online Closed Beta Test Trial Report

Last weekend, the most anticipated Dragon Ball Online released its grand closed beta test in South Korea for the first time. Some common players have been invited to take part in that test. Now, let's take a quick look at the trial report

Because of the high popularity of the original Dragon Ball comics, the MMO version has attracted a lot of attentions from players all over the world. Although the game has been announced two years ago, it is just fully presented to players this time.

This test lasted for only three days and the servers were available for a limited time. Plus, the game is Korean version, which may cause some misunderstandings for the game experience, so please forgive us if this report says something wrong.(Due to the time limitation of the test, We just take a try of Human's Wushu Master and Namekian's Namek Dragon.)

Dragon Ball Online offers three playable races and Dragon Ball Online Gold. Players can earn Exp to level up, while the Exp increase is connected with basic status and skill points. Each class possesses a unique skill tree and respective class feature will become more striking in pace with the development of a character.

Like other online games, in Dragon Ball Online, players can move around the world by using the W, A, S, D keys or just by using their mouse and jump by hitting the Space key. The character won't suffer any damage when jumping from a high position.

Right click on an enemy and operate the Shortcut Bar to begin a combat. The current game version has provided a variety of powerful skills, including attack skill, healing skill and buff assistant skills.
