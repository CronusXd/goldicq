Myth Angels Online Launched Open Beta with Tempting Prizes

USERJOY Technology announce that Myth Angels Online launches Open Beta Test .To celebrate the grand opening of MAO, USERJOY has prepared many awesome prizes, such as $5000 Cash, 50 Million Silver Coins, 30 Thousand Gold Coins, and more for gamers to claim! What's more, in order to welcome back old friends who used to join CBT, gamers will receive tempting rewards for which USERJOY specially prepared. Intrigued? Come and join up now!

Myth Angels Online is a Japanese anime style MMORPG based on Greek Mythology which stresses on its easy control and the excitement for gamers to blow away Bosses and monsters in the instance dungeons with pet troop and powerful robot. Besides the fun and exciting gameplay in MAO, USERJOY decides to spice things up by providing $5000 Cash reward which gamers will have chance to win by logging in Myth Angels Online daily. Want to take $5000 Cash home with you? Come and learn more HERE! That is not it, you will also have chance to win, to name a few, 50 Million Silver Coins and 30 Thousand Gold Coins, by joining this event. Come and see our PRIZES!

For those old friends who had joined CBT of Myth Angels Online, USERJOY has not forgotten you! In order to express our appreciation for your support, as long as you join OBT and create a character before Nov. you will receive a CB Souvenir Hat and 100 bottles of Regular Red and Blue Potion. What is more, those who had recharged angel gold in advance during CBT, don't forget that you will receive the 10% bonus on Open Beta Date!

Myth Angels Online (Myth Angels Gold) is the newest title of the Angels Online series developed by USERJOY. MAO has kept the original 2D cute style anime flavor of AO, and presents whole new game features, such as instance dungeon mode and pet troop, to gamers.

Based on Greek Mythology, gamers act as warriors called by the gods and goddesses to fight in the battles against evil forces. Gamers can go solo or with their friends to explore the Myth and enjoy the arcade-type excitement to wiping out monsters in the instance dungeons. Besides, gamers can raise various adorable pets to create their own pet troop, and ride on powerful robots to conquer the battles.
