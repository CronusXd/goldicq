The 250 V.S. 250 PVP Released Soon

For old friends who had joined CBT of Myth Angels Online, don't forget to come back to create a character before Nov. 9th 21:00 (PDT), you will receive a CB Souvenir Hat and 100 bottles of Regular Red and Blue Potion.

USERJOY Technology, an experienced free-to-play developer and publisher of MMO games, is excited to announce that the new system of Myth Angels Online, LARGE SCALE PvP event - Red-Blue Competition is going to be launched soon. The first round of Red-Blue Competition will be available on November 5th 3:00AM - 4:00AM and on November 6th 13:00PM - 14:00PM (PDT). Wait too long for a real fight? Want to prove you are the ONE? The answers are in the Red-Blue Competition!You will also have chance to win $5000 Myth Angels Gold by log in the game daily. Don't miss your big chance to win!

Red-Blue Competition is a 250 V.S. 250 large scale PVP event in Myth Angels Online. Gamers act as great fighters in ancient Greek Mythology whom are divided into two teams by Prometheus to retrieve and exterminate evil power of the Essence of Disaster which is released from Pandora's Box. Besides the plenty of experience rewards to the attendees of Red-Blue Competition, the TOP 3 players on both teams will receive EXTRA rewards from the system.

About Myth Angels Online

Myth Angels Online (MAO) is the newest title of the Angels Online series developed by USERJOY. MAO has kept the original 2D cute style anime flavor of AO, and presents whole new game features, such as instance dungeon mode and pet troop, to gamers.

Based on Greek Mythology, gamers act as warriors called by the gods and goddesses to fight in the battles against evil forces. Gamers can go solo or with their friends to explore the Myth and enjoy the arcade-type excitement to wiping out monsters in the instance dungeons. Besides, gamers can raise various adorable pets to create their own pet troop, and ride on powerful robots to conquer the battles.
