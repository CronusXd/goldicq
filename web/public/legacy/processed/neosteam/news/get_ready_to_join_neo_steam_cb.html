Get Ready to Join Neo Steam CB

NeoSteam Gold is a Steam punk Massively Multiplayer Online Role Playing Game developed by Joyimpact and published by Hanbitsoft.

NeoSteam Gold is a full 3D game with lush graphics that will surely enhance your gaming experience. You can choose from four different races and also contains 4 jobs with sub jobs for class diversity. Steam rides are also one of the best features of the game. You can choose from the basic Steam balloon to Steam Submarine to Steam Zeppellin and more!

The story mainly revolves around 3 kingdoms at war for the Neosteam gold. Each kingdom will have the chance to own Rope Isle, the largest Neosteam gold source. Join and be part of the Neo Steam world and let the Steam Wars begin!

Please stand by! And always visit the NeoSteam Gold website as the registration for the Closed Beta will be opening any time from now!

Closed Beta events are already lined up for all the beta testers of the game. Prepare yourselves to harness the power of the steam as Hanbitsoft and JoyImpact teamed-up once again to bring you another exciting MMORPG that will surely change the your gaming experience. Get ready to experience NeoSteam Gold!

Note: The schedule for the CBT is still tentative and can still be readjusted to suit all the testers.

Introduction

Neo Steam is a massively multiplayer online NeoSteam Online Gold role-playing game that relives the 19th century Steam Punk genre into a new and greater height! Mesmerize yourselves with Neo Steam's exciting full 3D graphics and animation. Be prepared to roam into the world filled with never before seen monsters with sizes you won't imagine.

Get to your destination with style as you roam around the world of NeoSteam Gold using different steam powered transportation machines. Finally, determine which side will you choose as the epic Steam Wars is about to be unleashed.
