DFO Guide for Execute Range

Execute range of DFO is easier than your normal rotation. It is a simple priority queue: Maintain Rend. Overpower. Execute. And that is it. Pop trinkets in unison as they are available and continue to use Bladestorm where appropriate. If you need to AoE, Bladestorm is obviously a good choice. You want to pop it with Sweeping Strikes up. Of course, Bladestorm is on a 1.5 minute cooldown, so if everything did not die during the 6s duration, or if it is on CD, you have to get a bit more creative.

First, you want to keep Sweeping Strikes on cooldown whenever you are AoEing, it is a significant DPS increase. From there, maintain your normal rotation on a single mob, but use Thunderclap every cooldown. This will not be a DPS if you are still in level(dungeon figher gold) gear, only use it if you are in quality stuff or if there are a huge number of mobs. We should point out that you do not want to apply Rend to as many mobs as possible, just leave it on one or you will lose DPS. Now there's a little AoE DPS technique we enjoy employing that you probably should not admit to doing, if you employ it at all. You see, Arms Warriors(dungeon figher online gold) have a certain ability that makes it easy to peel off rage buddies for cleave-spamming goodness. Say hello to our little friend: Mocking Blow. If you have faith in your healers and the mobs will not clobber you, you can get away with using Mocking Blow to snap a target on to you for a temporary rage boost that should allow you to use Cleave much more frequently.
