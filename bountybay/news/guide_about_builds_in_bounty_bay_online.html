Guide about builds in Bounty Bay Online

When you level you get Stat points in Bounty Bay Online, putting it in that gives you more physical damage and more hit points. Putting it on into gives you more magical damage and more manna points.

As you may have guessed putting all your stat points makes you Pure Strength and putting them all on into makes you are Pure Intelligence. As we said before it is a mixture and Str. Although this is the builds section, all the various builds and what skills are required recommended and more is under the skill points section as personal think it is best suited there.

When you use Bounty Bay Online Coin in the game, and you will find that how interesting it is. You can equip your creature with item cards. Your creature must have learned the Unit Control skill in order to equip item cards. The higher the skill level of Unit Control is, the more item cards you can equip on a creature.

Also, certain creature gets more bonuses depending on which kind of equipment they wear. To equip an item card, talk to the Tamer and choose Creature Formation. A familiar looking creature control window will open. Drag your item card onto an empty slot for your creature and it has done. You can do just the opposite as well, by equipping the creature card on yourself! You must equip a belt in order to do so. Just drag the creature card onto your belt slot to equip.

Different stat bonus is given depending on the creature Card skill. You can trade the creature cards with other players without any sort of penalty. Despite the rumors, there is no loyalty factor or anything like that. They will have their full stat and skills up to the level you have raised them when you give it to someone else.
