Game Description and Comments

Tibia is a free fantasy-themed MMORPG with very basic 2D graphics. As can be seen in the game's reviews, Tibia's graphics do turn away some gamers. However, this game is more than just about graphics. Like RuneScape, Tibia Gold is about gameplay and a huge gaming population. For example, there are currently over 25,000 players on 72 worlds as of this writing (early morning). The basic graphics and Buy Tibia Goldsuccess of the game have allowed it to offer a Tibia Gold Micro Edition on cell phones. The game is nothing mind-blowing, but instead is a solid fantasy MMO with a huge community. 

Key Features

Several different game worlds allow you to choose the one that best fits your play style.
Special events such as surprise attacks on cities out of nowhere.
Very small download.
Over 100,000 total players insures that you never have to play alone.
Premium features available.
Tutor system allows veteran players to help out beginners.

Author: MMORPG Fan
Rating: 5
Comment: I have played this game for about 3 years now and I have always found it fun to play. There are many different servers to chose from with different set ups. There are also PvP enforced servers that are very good for blood thirsty members of the public who just wish to play and kill. You gain exp for killing people of a higher level than you! Most worlds have wonderful communities where people will be willing to help you and it is extremely easy to make friends with people from all around the world. This game often reaches over 50 thousand people at one time!
