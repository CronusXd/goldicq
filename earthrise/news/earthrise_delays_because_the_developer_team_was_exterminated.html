Earthrise Delays Because the Developer Team was Exterminated

Post apocalyptic sci-fi MMORPG Earthrise will delay its launch, the developer Masthead told us the news yesterday. They told the news in a funny way, an interesting story, I should say, which makes the sad news to be a little bit entertaining. What happened to the Earthrise team, you will find the answer below.

In the office, Masthead team including Atanas are reading the "Jaberwokie"(the robot in the picture) letter. The team are in surprise and worry, the letter could be a ultimatum.

Than the evil robot assassin "Jaberwokie" getting aggro on the Masthead team. Jaberwokie is going to release some kind of skill or power, the team are in danger.

In a second, the Masthead team is wiped out by the evil robot assassin. What a terrible scene, is that in the movie SAW?

This message is coming to you from the safety of our remote emergency office, as the few survivors have fled the main premises and are in safe, unknown locations in deep seclusion recovering from the recent accidental attack of evil robot assassin "Jaberwokie". Due to the demolition of key figures in Masthead development, it has unavoidably slowed down progress, delaying development and quite possibly resulting in a later than originally envisioned launch schedule. (Oops.) The good news is that we still have forums, and therefore an outside link to all of our caring and concerned community where will we continue to provide updates regarding development progress as we rebuild and repair operations so we may continue bringing the world of Enterra to life. As proof of our commitment to you, we have provided a picture of our sys admin who will keep the forums alive at all costs.If you have no Earthrise Credits,you will be fail in the game Earthrise.

The determined survivors would also like me to mention that the combat video will not appear before the 16th of April due to this tragic circumstance. It will be released in full "when it is ready", as the robot assassin truly went out of control. We understand this delay places us at risk from sea bass, but we are not afraid! Our devotion is strengthened by the challenges ahead of us, and we thank you for your patience and understanding!
