Fiesta Leveling Guide Levels 60-88

Level 78

Still spam that Monster Collector quest. The few quests you will actually WANT to do are; A Letter from Bran 1. Pick up the second part and keep it. Keep all other quests.

Level 79

Spam Slay the Fire Nixes, which tells you to kill 150 fire nix. Youre gonna want to do only one other quests if you can; The Soldier of Destroy 4. Pick up Part 5 and keep it. Keep all other quests.

Level 80

You now have Contacting Wing Wing, to kill 200 Core Guardians. You still have the Nix one, but compared to Cores, its too low exp. So spam out the Cores and save the last Nix. Save all quests.

Level 81

Not only do you still have Contacting Wing Wing, but you now also have I Despise Moles, calling fo you to kill 200 Moles. This can get tricky.. Get a party at cores/moles, and spam it out. If you end up with twice as many cores as moles, then go ahead and turn in the cores. But if youre moles is 50 or less behind the cores, wait til it finishes, and turn in both. Save all other quests.

Level 82

Same as 81.

Level 83

Youve now lost the Cores quest. All you have left is Moles. So go to G6 in BR and try to get a Moles-only party. It goes much faster. Again; save all other quests. If youve hit 20/20, thats ok, youre gonna use em soon anyway.

Level 84

All those quests you saved? Yeah. Do them. Turn them all in. You should hit 85 with 6-10 left over, depending on which you do. Always do the easiest ones first.

Level 85

Get a Fire Nix license, and do a lic for your weapon. If you have a bk +9, do it for that. If you have an 80+9 and a BK+0, the 80 still does more dmg. Even a lv 70 +9 does more dmg than a lv 85+0. Pick the one you wanna do and do the lic. You should get 10-20 % out of it. Then do all your quests (This includes the Dragon Relic quest, which Ive heard gives you 25~50%. It may or may not level you, but it definitely helps). If you cant level after those.. well.. you do have the Kidmon repeat.. 2-3% per run isnt much, but its better than grinding in GHS.

Level 86

Trees trees and more trees. Tree of Jewels repeat, defeat 100 Jewel Trees. Do your other quests first, and spam Trees until you level.

Level 87

Same as 86.

Level 88

You get A Tree with Holes, killing 80 Red Nix in Flaming Mines. Spam the crud out of this until you finally... FINALLY.. cap/hit 89.
