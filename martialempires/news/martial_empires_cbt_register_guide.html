Martial Empires CBT Register Guide

I posted an article about the beta event of Martial Empires yesterday, but some player still can NOT access the closed beta, including me. So I report this problem to Gamigo immediately. Below is their feedback.

The user should make sure that they created an English account. Many users tried to use the beta key with a german account and that doesn’t work because its an English beta. Our technicians investigate if there are more problems we don’t know about yet.

After reading the feedback, I tried again and then my Martial Empires account did work. Here is the surefire way for getting an available game account:

Step 1: Register from the home page of Martial Empires official website.

 

Step 2: Login from Gamigo's account support page, using the account you've register from the first step.

Step 3: Create a game account for Martial Empires.

Step 4: Sign in MMOsite and get key from here.

Step 5: Enter your beta test key and Activate your game account on Martial Empires' Beta Page.

Now you can start your Martial Empires journey after installing the client.
