The Guide for Anarchy Online

No matter if it's the Plane of Time, level 70 experience groups or raid pulling - Sampson has really seen a lot of Anarchy, analysed dozens of end-level encounters.

Fought in some of the most challenging zone and soloed for a little fortune. In his Anarchy monk guide he now shares his knowledge with you:

Highlights of the Anarchy Credits Monk Guide:
A cutting-edge pulling guide
Strategies and locations that let your monk level twice as fast
How monks can make a fortune
An assiduous analysis of the mechanics behind the monk class
Disciplines, skills and how to apply them best
Alternate advancement and how to do it properly
How to get deadly equipment with hardly any effort
Monk quest walk-throughs including short cuts

Pulling: The ultimate Anarchy Credits pulling chapter explains simple and advanced tactics for grouped and raid encounters.

Step by step instructions include graphical displays of pulling tactics and guide you through the whole process: From optimizing preparations and the actual pulling process to increasing your safety and that of your group and guild mates.

Learn how to pull raid encounters, get to know different splitting techniques and have the details of lull and caster pulling explained to you. The precisely written guideline will gain you the respect of group and guild mates alike.

Experience: Hints and tactics for solo and grouped players should get your monk to level 70 in no time. Sampson explains different camps for each level range and provides you with a list of recommended fighting spots.

Platinum: Monks haven't got an easy time when it comes to making AO Credits. This guide, however, provides you with monk-friendly camp spots and a guideline that makes sure that you don't run out of platinum.

Learn how to fill up your purse while making excellent experience.

Creation guide: Iksar monk or human monk? Short question, long answer: Exact regeneration data for each level, weight limits, experience modifiers, fist damage/delay statistics, indirect influence factors and questing consequences.

This chapter makes sure that you not only make the right but an educated choice when creating a new monk in Anarchy Credits.
