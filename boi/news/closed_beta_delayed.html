Closed Beta Delayed

Battle of the Immortals, has been pushed back to to mid-April. The beta, which was originally scheduled to go live today, was delayed as a result of the developers wanting more time to clean up and polish the game. The Perfect World Entertainment blog goes into more details on the delay, including some of the things players can look forward to in the Closed Beta as a result of the extra time: The following is a list of changes that are scheduled to occur prior to this new Closed Beta: - Support for Windows 7 - Support for 64-bit systems - Support for higher monitor resolutions - Fully integrated in-game video tutorial system - Revised cash shop structure and items - Revised quest flow for low level players - Revised monster AI - Readjusted damage and HP for both players and monsters - Revised dungeon loot tables The fact of the matter is the developers simply weren''''t happy with where the game was at, and they required more time to polish and clean the game up before they are ready for testers to begin putting it through its paces.

Soul Gear is one of the game most standout features, as Soul Gear evolves with your character, and is very highly animated. Dragon Emperor Catacombs also features easy and normal modes, the former is meant for players level 40-45, and has a diminished chance of dropping Soul Gear. Normal mode is for players 45+, and has a higher chance to drop Soul Gear. Once you hit level 60 you''''ll have access to the Spectre Island dungeon, which pits you against tons of undead pirates. The dungeon is strewn with many mini bosses and tons of lucrative quests. Like Dragon Emperor Catacombs, Spectre Island includes easy and normal mode, as well as an additional elite mode. Elite mode provides an even higher chance to drop Soul Gear and allows players to fight the dungeons final boss again once they reset the dungeon, giving them two chances at awesome loot.
