Gaia Online Update Preview

The next bit was about the upcoming manga story line. They went over screen shots of black and white, less-dialog, pages from the forthcoming story. The artwork was dynamic and exciting. Lanzer, founder of Gaia Online took the time to explain how the pages are created. He spoke of the technical details in overview in how the process of creating these pages. Then next was news of a new NPCs, items, and pets. (Cathulu!!)

In another planned update the mini game Gaia Tiles will be released. This game will involve matching chains of tiles and clearing boards. The requirements for each level are based on how many tiles in a chain and how many chains you need to complete. Chains are made by matching backgrounds or foregrounds on the tiles. For completing the levels you will earn tickets which you can use for prizes or sell them in the marketplace for coin.

They announced a new feature called “Dumpster Dive”. Right now when players trash items from their inventory they are gone for good. After this update those deleted items will be placed in the dumpster for other players to search through. Item rewards from the dumpster are random though. After you find an item you can send a note to the user who trashed it thanking them for the donation.You must buy gaia online gold in the whole game. Then you can buy more and more useful things to protect yourself.

The event was a blast and everyone had a great time, Gaia Online really drives that whole community feeling home. During the course of the event people at home were allowed to participate as well, this really brought it all together. So if you participate in Gaia Online, look for these changes coming down the pipe.
